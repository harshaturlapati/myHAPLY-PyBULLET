    Equation lab

    Write your own force law, check what it does, feel it on the device, and propose it as a new built-in behaviour. Forces are in newtons, positions in metres, velocities in m/s.

    Your equations

    Variables: x y z (m), vx vy vz (m/s), t (s), the target xd yd zd and the slider values kx ky kz (N/m), bx by bz (N·s/m). Functions: sin cos tan exp log sqrt abs sign step floor min max pow atan2 clamp; step(a) is 1 when a > 0.
